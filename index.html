Legal Templates UK

Browser-only starter letters for common consumer, housing, data, education, travel, accessibility, banking, and council problems.

Safety note

These templates are practical drafting aids, not legal advice. Check deadlines, local procedures, contract terms, appeal windows, and whether specialist advice is needed.

Catalogue

Refund request

Consumer Rights Act remedy request for goods, services, or digital content.

Chargeback or bank complaint

Ask a bank to assess chargeback, Section 75, or its complaint process.

Landlord repair notice

Report disrepair, ask for inspection and repair dates, and keep evidence.

Subject access request

Request personal data and supplementary information under UK data protection law.

University adjustment request

Ask for disability adjustments for exams, teaching, placements, or student services.

Rail delay compensation

Claim Delay Repay or Passenger Charter compensation for delayed journeys.

Airline assistance complaint

Complain about special assistance, mobility-aid handling, or airport support failures.

Council escalation

Escalate unresolved service failures through a council complaint process.

Contribute

Add templates with official or specialist public-interest sources, plain-English safety notes, tests, and no backend dependency. Avoid example content that includes real account numbers, medical records, or private addresses.
