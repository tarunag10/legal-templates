# Legal Templates UK

Plain-English legal and civic letter templates for common UK problems. The starter catalogue covers refund requests, chargeback or bank complaints, landlord repair notices, subject access requests, university adjustment requests, rail delay compensation, airline assistance complaints, and council complaint escalation.

## Demo

Open `index.html` in a browser. This repository is intentionally no-backend and keeps user data local to the browser. Template text is rendered by local JavaScript only; there is no tracking, account, API call, database, or form submission.

## Catalogue

- Refund request: Consumer Rights Act 2015 remedy wording.
- Chargeback or bank complaint: card dispute, chargeback, Section 75, and Financial Ombudsman escalation prompts.
- Landlord repair notice: disrepair report, inspection request, evidence list, and access wording.
- Subject access request: UK GDPR personal-data request wording.
- University adjustment request: disability adjustment request for teaching, exams, placements, or student services.
- Rail delay compensation: Delay Repay, Passenger Charter, and National Rail Conditions of Travel prompts.
- Airline assistance complaint: accessibility and special-assistance complaint wording.
- Council escalation: formal complaint escalation and Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman prompt.

The browser UI includes category filtering, text search across template metadata, dynamic catalogue cards, local copy support, `.txt` downloads with safe generated filenames, browser-local favourite templates, favourites-first ordering, Markdown downloads for templates, and copyable local action packs with evidence, safety, next-step, and escalation prompts. The eight starter templates remain the baseline catalogue.

## Source checks

Starter wording is informed by public guidance from GOV.UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, National Rail, the Office of Rail and Road, the UK Civil Aviation Authority, the Financial Ombudsman Service, and Shelter England. Contributors should prefer official or specialist public-interest sources, and should record any deadline or territory limits in the template safety note.

## Safety note

This project provides information and drafting support, not legal advice. Users should check deadlines, local rules, complaint routes, appeal windows, limitation periods, and professional advice where needed. Avoid putting full account numbers, medical records, or other unnecessary sensitive detail in shared examples.
